# Streetwise Connection — Business Model

## Identity

**Streetwise Connection℠**  
**Technology • Connectivity • Cybersecurity**

**Core promise:** Technology solutions that connect, support, and secure the people and businesses that depend on them.

## Business model

Streetwise Connection is a technology services company serving individuals, households, small businesses, and organisations. Services may be delivered on-site or remotely.

### Four divisions

1. **Connect** — internet, Wi-Fi, networking, routers, access points, and network troubleshooting.
2. **Support** — computers, phones, tablets, software, setup, troubleshooting, migration, backup, and technical guidance.
3. **Secure** — defensive cybersecurity, account security, MFA, password management, device hardening, privacy, and security reviews.
4. **Business** — small-business IT setup, cloud/workspace configuration, device deployment, security, documentation, and ongoing support.

Remote delivery is a method used across all four divisions, not a separate division.

## Revenue

- **One-time services:** defined support or configuration jobs.
- **Projects:** larger installations, migrations, deployments, and redesigns.
- **Recurring services:** technology support, network care, and small-business IT support plans.

## Customer markets

### Consumer
Individuals, households, remote workers, and home technology users.

### Business
Small businesses, start-ups, independent professionals, home-based businesses, and small organisations.

## Geographic strategy

- Phase 1: Las Vegas / Southern Nevada
- Phase 2: United States
- Phase 3: remote international services

The brand should not be positioned as a Las Vegas-only repair shop.

## Service boundaries

Streetwise initially provides practical defensive technology services. It does not advertise advanced penetration testing, digital forensics, incident response, regulated telecom services, or enterprise-scale 24/7 managed security until the necessary contracts, tooling, procedures, insurance, authorisation controls, and capabilities exist.

## Future connectivity products

The existing cellular/eSIM work in this repository is retained as a **future product/technical foundation**, not the primary public business model. Any carrier/MVNO product remains gated by provider, legal, regulatory, technical, security, and economics requirements.
