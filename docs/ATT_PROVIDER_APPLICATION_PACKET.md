## 3. Business description for AT&T

Short version:

Streetwise Connection is preparing a branded cellular service for residential customers and small businesses. Streetwise intends to provide customer account management, first-line support, compatibility and activation assistance, plan administration, security-focused account controls and other permitted services around approved wholesale wireless products.

Long version:

Streetwise Connection is a pre-launch cellular service business seeking an approved AT&T reseller or wholesale relationship for recurring U.S. wireless connectivity. The intended product may include mobile data, hotspot, voice, SMS, local phone numbers, number portability, SIM/eSIM activation, 5G, VoLTE, Wi-Fi calling and roaming where the final AT&T agreement supports those capabilities. Streetwise will own its customer experience and fulfil Tier 1 support obligations required by the agreement. Streetwise will not advertise AT&T-backed service, charge customers for AT&T-supported cellular service or activate live lines until commercial, regulatory, technical and production gates are complete.
