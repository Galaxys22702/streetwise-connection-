# Streetwise Connection — Privacy Policy (Pre-Launch Draft)

> **Draft only. Not effective until formally adopted and published.** Review against the final production architecture, providers, and applicable law before launch.

**Last draft update:** August 25, 2026

Streetwise Connection ("Streetwise," "we," "us," or "our") is developing a platform for eSIM-based mobile-data services. This draft explains the categories of information the service is expected to process and the purposes for that processing.

## 1. Information we may collect

### Account information

We may collect information such as:

- email address;
- account identifier;
- authentication and session information;
- account creation and login records.

Passwords should be stored only as cryptographic password hashes. Session credentials should be protected and should not be logged or exposed publicly.

### Service and eSIM information

We may process information needed to provide mobile-data service, including:

- selected plan and country;
- device description supplied by the customer;
- eSIM order identifiers and activation status;
- installation information such as ICCID, SM-DP+ address, or activation data when necessary to deliver the service;
- usage and allowance information supplied by the network/eSIM provider.

### Payment and subscription information

Payments may be processed by a third-party payment processor. Streetwise should avoid storing full payment-card numbers or security codes. We may receive transaction, checkout, subscription, refund, dispute, and payment-status information from the processor.

### Technical and security information

We may process limited technical information needed to operate and secure the service, such as request metadata, error logs, fraud indicators, security events, and service-health information.

## 2. How information may be used

Information may be used to:

- create and secure accounts;
- authenticate users;
- process subscriptions and payments;
- provision and manage eSIM/mobile-data service;
- display service status, installation details, and usage;
- provide customer support;
- prevent fraud, abuse, duplicate orders, and unauthorized access;
- maintain, debug, and improve the platform;
- comply with legal, tax, regulatory, and contractual obligations.

## 3. Service providers

Streetwise may share information with vendors only as reasonably necessary to operate the service. Expected categories include:

- payment processors;
- wholesale eSIM/mobile-network providers;
- cloud hosting and database providers;
- email/support providers;
- security, monitoring, and fraud-prevention providers;
- professional advisers and government authorities when legally required.

The final published policy should identify important providers where appropriate and should match the executed contracts and actual production data flows.

## 4. Sale of personal information

Streetwise should not sell customer personal information as a business model. If the business model changes in a way that legally constitutes a sale or sharing of personal information, this policy and required consumer rights processes must be updated before that activity begins.

## 5. Data retention

Streetwise should retain personal information only for as long as reasonably needed for service delivery, security, accounting, disputes, legal obligations, regulatory requirements, and legitimate business records. Specific retention periods should be documented before launch.

## 6. Security

Streetwise uses or intends to use safeguards such as encrypted HTTPS transport, password hashing, token hashing, restricted production credentials, security headers, database access controls, logging controls, and provider safety switches. No system can guarantee absolute security.

## 7. Customer choices and rights

The final policy should provide a contact method for customers to request access, correction, deletion, or other rights available under applicable law. Some information may need to be retained to satisfy legal, security, fraud, accounting, or contractual requirements.

## 8. Children

The service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. The final Terms should specify any minimum account age required for purchasing service.

## 9. International use

Because eSIM services may be used while traveling, information may be processed by network and service providers in multiple jurisdictions. The final policy should describe cross-border processing based on the actual provider contracts and launch countries.

## 10. Changes

Streetwise may update the policy as the service, vendors, or legal requirements change. Material changes should be communicated as required by law.
