# SINGLE-MEMBER OPERATING AGREEMENT
## Streetwise Connection LLC

> **Draft for review before adoption.** This document is not effective until the LLC has been formed, the blanks are completed, and the member signs it. It is not a substitute for advice from a Nevada attorney or tax professional.

This Operating Agreement (the **Agreement**) is adopted effective **[DATE]** by **Robert Tursi** (the **Member**) for **Streetwise Connection LLC** (the **Company**), a Nevada limited-liability company.

### 1. Formation

The Company will be formed under Chapter 86 of the Nevada Revised Statutes by filing Articles of Organization with the Nevada Secretary of State. The Company will maintain all state and local registrations required for its actual activities.

### 2. Name

The legal name of the Company is **Streetwise Connection LLC**, subject to acceptance by the Nevada Secretary of State. The Company may use approved trade names or fictitious firm names as permitted by law.

### 3. Principal Office

The principal business office will be located in Las Vegas, Nevada, at an address maintained in the Company's private records. The public software repository will not be used to store the Member's private residence address or sensitive filing information.

### 4. Registered Agent

The Company will continuously maintain a Nevada registered agent as required by law. The registered-agent name and address will be maintained in official company records and state filings.

### 5. Purpose

The Company may engage in any lawful business permitted to a Nevada limited-liability company, including developing and operating software, internet and telecommunications platforms, eSIM/mobile-data resale, customer account and billing systems, technology services, and related lawful activities, subject to required licenses and regulatory approvals.

### 6. Sole Member and Ownership

The Company is initially owned one hundred percent (100%) by **Robert Tursi**. No other person receives an ownership interest unless the Member approves the change in a written amendment, the Company's ownership records are updated, and any required government, banking, tax, contractual, or regulatory notices are completed.

### 7. Capital Contributions

The Member may contribute cash, property, intellectual property, services, or other value to the Company. Contributions will be documented in the Company's private accounting records. No contribution creates an obligation to make additional contributions unless agreed in writing.

### 8. Management

The Company will be member-managed unless the Member later adopts a written amendment providing otherwise. The Member has authority to manage the Company's business, enter contracts, open accounts, hire personnel or contractors, acquire services, and take other lawful actions on behalf of the Company.

### 9. Separate Company Finances

The Company will maintain business funds separately from personal funds. Company revenue and expenses should flow through accounts maintained in the Company's name once such accounts are available. The Member will keep reasonable books and records sufficient for tax, licensing, contractual, and regulatory obligations.

### 10. Contracts and Spending Authority

The Member may sign contracts and authorize expenditures for the Company. Material agreements involving telecommunications providers, payment processing, debt, equity ownership, intellectual-property transfers, or long-term financial commitments should be retained in the Company's permanent records.

### 11. Telecommunications Compliance

The Company will not represent itself as holding spectrum rights, carrier authority, or other licenses it does not possess. Before live telecommunications service is sold, the Company will determine and satisfy applicable Nevada PUCN, FCC, USAC, local, tax, consumer-protection, privacy, and contractual requirements. Live eSIM provisioning will remain subject to the Company's provider agreements and launch controls.

### 12. Intellectual Property

Software, documentation, branding, business processes, domain names, and other intellectual property acquired or created for the Company should be documented as Company property when legally appropriate. Personal intellectual property is not transferred to the Company unless documented in writing.

### 13. Liability and Indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by Nevada law, the Company will indemnify the Member and authorized agents for actions taken in good faith on behalf of the Company. Nothing in this Agreement authorizes fraud, willful misconduct, unlawful conduct, or acts outside the protection permitted by law.

### 14. Tax Treatment

Unless a different election is properly made, the Company is intended to use the default federal tax classification applicable to a single-member LLC. Tax elections will be made only after considering their legal and financial consequences.

### 15. Records

The Company will maintain required records, including formation documents, ownership records, tax records, material contracts, licenses, regulatory filings, financial records, and amendments to this Agreement. Sensitive records will not be stored in a public source-code repository.

### 16. Adding a Member

A second member or other owner may be admitted only through a written amendment stating the ownership percentages, capital contributions, voting rights, management rights, profit/loss allocation, transfer restrictions, buyout terms, and effective date. Verbal discussions about partnership do not change ownership.

### 17. Transfers

The Member may not treat an ownership transfer as complete until the transfer is documented in writing and all required company, tax, contractual, banking, licensing, and regulatory records are updated.

### 18. Dissolution

The Company may be dissolved by the Member or as otherwise required by Nevada law. On dissolution, liabilities will be addressed before remaining assets are distributed to the Member.

### 19. Amendments

The Member may amend this Agreement in writing. Each amendment should be dated and retained with the Company's permanent records.

### 20. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Nevada.
