## Objective

Make a no-cost first contact with AT&T Partner Exchange to determine the correct reseller / wholesale path for Streetwise Connection before spending money on provider onboarding.

## Recommended first route

Use the official AT&T Partner Exchange **Request info** form:

https://www.business.att.com/industries/partner-solutions/att-partner-exchange.html

AT&T currently describes Partner Exchange as a reseller program with customer-lifecycle control, co-branding, Tier 1 support responsibilities, tools/APIs, and a resale portfolio that includes wireless voice, data, and messaging.

Keep AT&T Wholesale as the parallel/fallback route:

https://www.business.att.com/industries/wholesale.html

- first name;
- last name;
- email address;
- phone;
- company name;
- comment.

Use **Streetwise Connection** as the company name until Nevada legally accepts `Streetwise Connection LLC`.
