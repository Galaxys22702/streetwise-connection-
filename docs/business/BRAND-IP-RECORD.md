## Important first-use limitation

Streetwise is currently in **waitlist/pre-launch mode**. Public display, a waitlist, repository history, and use of the ℠ symbol create useful evidence of brand adoption, but this record **does not declare a first-use-in-commerce date for live cellular/telecommunications services**.

When Streetwise first lawfully renders the claimed service to a real customer in U.S. commerce, separately record:

- exact date of first qualifying service;
- service actually provided;
- customer/order record or invoice reference kept privately;
- webpage or advertising specimen tied to that service;
- geographic area;
- provider/wholesale relationship in effect at the time.

Do not backdate or invent a first-use date.
