# Streetwise Connection — Product Positioning

## Core position

**Affordable cellular service without the usual confusion.**

Streetwise Connection is being designed as a cellular/MVNO brand for residential customers and small businesses. The product should combine affordable service with security-focused account controls, clear plan terms, practical setup/support, and better resilience options than a basic low-cost plan.

The differentiator is not merely price. Cheap cellular service is already crowded. Streetwise should earn attention by making the service safer, clearer, easier to manage, and harder to lose when a single connection path fails.

## Product pillars

### 1. Affordable cellular

Streetwise should target straightforward monthly pricing and avoid surprise overages or deliberately confusing plan language.

### 2. Security-first service

Planned differentiators include:

- stronger account takeover protection
- SIM-swap controls and alerts
- phishing/scam and malicious-domain protection where technically supported
- secure account recovery
- device/line change notifications
- fraud and unusual-activity controls
- optional secure DNS or equivalent network protection where the provider/network design permits it

These are roadmap items until implemented and verified.

### 3. Connectivity resilience

Streetwise should pursue provider/network arrangements that allow better continuity when coverage is weak or a network path is unavailable.

This does not mean Streetwise can currently promise automatic carrier switching. Multi-network selection, failover, or profile switching must be supported by the provider contract, SIM/eSIM architecture, device, and implemented orchestration before it can be marketed.

### 4. Emergency connectivity reserve

A planned differentiator is a protected amount of connectivity reserved for essential uses such as messaging, maps, account access, and urgent communication after ordinary high-speed allowance is exhausted.

This feature depends on provider billing/plan controls and must not be advertised as available until technically proven and contractually permitted.

### 5. Cellular basics without feature games

The target full-cellular product should support, where the selected provider permits:

- mobile data
- hotspot
- voice
- SMS
- local mobile number
- number portability
- 5G
- VoLTE
- Wi-Fi calling
- SIM/eSIM lifecycle
- roaming/international options

### 6. Domestic plus international

Streetwise should separate recurring U.S. cellular service from travel connectivity. A domestic customer should not unknowingly depend on a travel-roaming profile that can become unsuitable for permanent same-country use.

Travel/global connectivity can remain an additional product path.

## Target customers

### Residential

Customers who want affordable cellular service, clear terms, account security, compatibility guidance, activation help, and practical support.

Planning target:

- Streetwise Home — $25/month

### Commercial / small business

Small teams that need multiple lines and central support without a large telecom or IT department.

Planning targets:

- Business Starter — $20/month per line
- Business Volume — $15/month per line for 3+ lines
- Business Pro — $30/month per line

These prices are planning targets only. Final retail plans require provider mapping, tax/surcharge analysis, support/fraud reserve, and contribution-margin validation.

## Service experience

Streetwise should aim to provide:

1. device and eSIM/SIM compatibility guidance before checkout;
2. clear disclosure of high-speed data, throttling, hotspot, roaming, voice/SMS, number and network terms;
3. supported activation and transfer/porting when available;
4. security controls that are visible to the customer instead of buried in support procedures;
5. residential and small-business plan paths;
6. multi-line administration for commercial customers;
7. practical troubleshooting and support;
8. transparent notices when a feature depends on a specific provider/network/device;
9. international/travel add-ons that do not compromise the domestic service model.

## Claims discipline

Do not advertise any capability until it is supported by the selected provider contract and the implemented system.

That includes:

- unlimited data
- unlimited hotspot
- guaranteed speeds
- guaranteed coverage
- automatic carrier switching
- emergency connectivity reserve
- scam/phishing filtering
- local phone numbers
- number portability
- voice/SMS
- Wi-Fi calling or VoLTE
- specific carrier access
- carrier or spectrum ownership

## Commercial principle

Streetwise should compete on **affordability + security + resilience + clarity + support**.

The repo should remain fail-closed: a capability can be designed in advance, but it cannot become a public promise merely because someone added a checkbox to a webpage.
