# Streetwise Connection — Product Positioning

## Core position

**Connection without the confusion.**

Streetwise Connection is being designed as an affordable connectivity service for residential customers and small businesses that combines plan access with practical setup guidance and support.

The differentiator is not merely lower price. Streetwise should reduce the friction that customers face when choosing, activating and managing connectivity.

## Target customers

### Residential

Customers who want a simple plan, clear limits, compatibility guidance and help getting connected.

Planned target price:

- Streetwise Home — **$25/month**

### Commercial / small business

Small teams that need multiple connected devices or lines but do not necessarily have dedicated IT staff.

Planned target prices:

- Business Starter — **$20/month per line**
- Business Volume — **$15/month per line for 3+ lines**
- Business Pro — **$30/month per line**

These prices are commercial targets only. They may not be sold until provider mapping, taxes, support exposure, refunds and contribution margin are validated.

## Service experience

Streetwise should aim to provide:

1. device compatibility guidance before checkout;
2. clear plan and usage terms;
3. setup and activation assistance;
4. residential and small-business plan paths;
5. multi-line planning for business customers;
6. practical troubleshooting and support;
7. a pre-purchase Connection Check that reduces failed activations and unsuitable purchases.

## Claims discipline

Do not advertise any of the following unless supported by written provider terms and the final approved launch plan:

- unlimited data;
- unlimited hotspot;
- guaranteed speeds;
- guaranteed coverage;
- carrier or spectrum ownership;
- features not mapped to the selected provider bundle.

Use factual language such as **data and hotspot included** only after the relevant provider terms are confirmed.

## Commercial principle

Streetwise should compete on **clarity + affordability + support**, not price alone.

A cheap plan that produces activation failures, poor support or negative contribution margin is not a viable product. Every launch plan must pass the repository's provider, compliance and economics gates before public checkout is enabled.
