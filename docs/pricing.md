# Streetwise Connection — Pricing Framework v1

Pricing is a framework until service scope, local operating costs, insurance, travel, tools, taxes, payment processing, and actual delivery times are validated.

## Pricing principles

1. Price the outcome and scope, not vague access to a technician.
2. Use fixed prices for simple repeatable services.
3. Quote larger or uncertain jobs after assessment.
4. Charge separately for hardware and third-party subscriptions unless explicitly included.
5. Remote services should generally be cheaper than equivalent on-site work when travel and physical access add no value.
6. Recurring plans should have clearly defined response times, included support, exclusions, and cancellation terms.
7. Never advertise a price as final if required third-party costs are unknown.

## Initial pricing architecture

### Connect
- Quick network assessment: fixed-price entry service.
- Wi-Fi optimisation: fixed-price service.
- Router/mesh setup: fixed-price service.
- Business network projects: assessment + written quote.

### Support
- Quick remote support: fixed-price session.
- Computer/device setup: fixed-price service.
- Data migration: starting price + scope adjustment where data volume or device complexity requires it.
- On-site support: service minimum + applicable travel/time charge.

### Secure
- Account/MFA setup: fixed-price service.
- Password-manager setup: fixed-price service.
- Device-security configuration: fixed-price service.
- Basic security assessment: assessment fee + written findings where appropriate.

### Business
- Business technology assessment: fixed-price assessment.
- New-business setup: project quote.
- Device deployment: per-device or project quote.
- Ongoing IT support: monthly plan with explicit scope and response target.

## Recurring-plan structure

### Streetwise Support
For individuals/households needing continuing remote technology assistance.

### Streetwise Network Care
For customers who want recurring network checks, maintenance, and troubleshooting.

### Streetwise Business IT
For small businesses needing ongoing technical support.

Exact dollar amounts should be published only after a delivery-time and cost review. The current public $29/$49/$69 phone-support prices belong to the previous cellular/mobile-setup model and should not be treated as the new company-wide pricing.
