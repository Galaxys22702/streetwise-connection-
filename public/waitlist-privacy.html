Waitlist privacy notice

This notice applies only to the Streetwise Connection launch waitlist. It is not the final service privacy policy.

What we collect

If the waitlist is open, we collect your email address and a timestamp/version showing that you agreed to receive launch updates.

How we use it

We use this information only to send Streetwise Connection launch updates. We do not request payment, government identification, device identifiers, eSIM activation information, or mobile-data usage through the waitlist.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell waitlist information. We keep it only as needed for launch communications, then delete or de-identify it according to the final retention schedule published before service begins.

Your choices

You can ask to be removed from the waitlist using the support contact shown on the waitlist page. The waitlist remains closed until that contact is configured.
