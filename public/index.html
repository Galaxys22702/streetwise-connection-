Cellular service in development

Affordable cellular service without the usual confusion.

Streetwise Connection is building a security-focused cellular service for households and small businesses. The goal is affordable mobile connectivity with clear terms, practical support, stronger account protection, and smarter resilience options. Service is not for sale yet. There is no checkout, payment, line activation, phone-number transfer, or live eSIM order available today.

Why Streetwise

Not just another cheap phone plan.

Low price matters, but Streetwise is being designed around the parts customers usually discover only after something goes wrong: security, support, clear service limits, line control, and connectivity resilience.

01

Affordable cellular

Planning for straightforward residential and small-business service with clear data, hotspot, voice, messaging, number and roaming terms before payment.

02

Security first

Planned account, SIM-swap, port-out, recovery and line-change protections, plus scam/phishing or malicious-domain protection where the final network design supports it.

03

Built for resilience

Streetwise is evaluating network and provider options that may support better continuity, international service and an emergency-connectivity reserve. These features remain in development until verified.

Planned cellular capabilities

A broader mobile service.

The long-term product is being designed around mobile data and hotspot plus full cellular features where the selected provider supports them.

01

Talk, text and data

Voice, SMS, mobile data, local-number support and number portability are target capabilities, not active services today.

02

Home and travel

Domestic U.S. service is being separated from travel connectivity so recurring customers are not forced onto a short-duration roaming model.

03

Small-business lines

Multi-line planning, central administration, deployment guidance and security controls are part of the commercial roadmap.
