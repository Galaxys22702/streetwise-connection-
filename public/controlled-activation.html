Controlled Activation

A line should not go live until the required commercial, technical and customer checks have passed.

What it means

Streetwise is being designed so live cellular activation remains disabled until the selected provider is approved, the applicable plan is mapped correctly, device and eSIM workflows are tested, billing is ready and the required launch gates are complete.

For customers

When live service becomes available, activation should be deliberate and traceable: the customer selects a supported plan, confirms the required information, completes the approved purchase flow and receives an activation only through the authorised provisioning path.

Current status

Public Streetwise cellular sales, live eSIM ordering and paid line activation remain locked today. Joining the waitlist does not activate a cellular line or create a paid mobile subscription.

Why it matters

Failing closed reduces the chance of accidental activations, unsupported plans, incorrect billing or provisioning against a provider that has not been commercially approved.
