Streetwise commitment

Controlled Activation

A Streetwise line should only go live through an authorised, verified activation path.

The commitment

Streetwise is being designed so a mobile line cannot become active simply because a customer clicked a button or because a backend request was received. Activation should occur only after the relevant commercial, technical, billing, compatibility and customer checks have passed.

Before Streetwise opens sales

Live activation remains disabled until Streetwise has an approved wholesale relationship, mapped provider products, tested eSIM or SIM provisioning, verified billing behaviour, documented customer support procedures, required disclosures and the applicable launch-readiness gates completed.

Customer authorisation

When live service launches, the activation flow should require the customer to intentionally choose a supported plan, provide the information required for that service, review the material terms, authorise payment where applicable and confirm the activation request. Streetwise will not treat a waitlist signup as consent to activate or bill a mobile line.

Device and eSIM checks

Where technically available, Streetwise intends to perform device compatibility and eSIM capability checks before provisioning. The purpose is to reduce failed activations and avoid issuing a profile that cannot reasonably be used on the intended device.

Provider-side provisioning

The actual network service must be created through the selected provider's authorised provisioning system. Streetwise's software will treat provider confirmation, order state and identifiers as part of the activation record rather than assuming that an API request succeeded.

Duplicate and accidental orders

Streetwise's platform is being designed with idempotency and order-state controls so the same customer action or repeated network request does not intentionally create duplicate paid services. Failed or uncertain provider responses should be reconciled instead of blindly retried.

Billing and activation

Billing and service state should stay aligned. A payment event by itself should not be treated as proof that a line is active, and an activation event should not be treated as proof that billing completed correctly. Streetwise intends to track these states separately and reconcile them before presenting a final customer status.

SIM changes, replacements and transfers

Future eSIM replacement, SIM swap, device-transfer and number-port workflows should require appropriate authentication and provider-supported checks. High-risk changes should not be processed as casually as ordinary profile edits.

What happens when something fails

If a required check fails or the provider returns an uncertain result, Streetwise's intended posture is to stop and investigate rather than force the activation through. The customer should receive an honest status such as pending, failed or needs support instead of a false “active” message.

Current status

Streetwise cellular sales, live eSIM ordering and paid line activation remain locked. The public website is currently a waitlist and planning experience. No customer joining the waitlist is purchasing or activating Streetwise cellular service.

Why controlled activation matters

Controlled activation reduces the risk of accidental charges, duplicate subscriptions, unsupported devices, unauthorised line changes, failed eSIM deliveries and provisioning against a provider or plan that Streetwise has not fully approved.

What it does not mean

Controlled activation cannot guarantee that every carrier-side activation will be instant or error-free. It means Streetwise will use deliberate checks, clear states and authorised provisioning paths rather than treating activation as an uncontrolled one-click action.
