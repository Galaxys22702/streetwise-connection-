Streetwise commitment

Clear Terms

Customers should be able to understand the real price, real limits and real service conditions before they pay.

The commitment

Streetwise will present the material terms of every live plan in plain language before checkout. A headline price or the word “unlimited” will not replace the details that determine how the service actually works.

Price and billing

For each live plan, Streetwise will disclose the recurring monthly price, whether taxes and regulatory fees are included or added, any activation or setup charge, the billing frequency, renewal behaviour, cancellation rules, refund rules where applicable, and any other material recurring or one-time charge known at the point of sale.

High-speed data

If a plan has a high-speed data allowance, Streetwise will state the allowance in GB and explain what happens after that amount is used. If speeds are reduced, the reduced-speed policy will be stated. If network management or deprioritisation can affect performance, that will be disclosed separately rather than being confused with a fixed throttle.

Hotspot and tethering

Streetwise will state whether hotspot or tethering is allowed, how much high-speed hotspot data is included, whether hotspot traffic is speed-limited, and what happens after the hotspot allowance is exhausted. If the upstream provider prohibits or restricts certain tethering use, that restriction will be disclosed.

“Unlimited” explained

If Streetwise uses the word “unlimited”, the same plan description will also identify any high-speed threshold, reduced-speed condition, hotspot limitation, video-management rule, deprioritisation condition, prohibited-use rule or other material network restriction that affects normal customer use. Streetwise will not rely on a vague footnote to change the practical meaning of the headline offer.

Network and coverage

Streetwise will explain the network relationship at the level it is contractually permitted to disclose. Coverage will be presented as expected service availability, not a guarantee that every device will receive a particular speed or signal at every location. Device compatibility, local congestion, buildings, terrain and network maintenance can affect performance.

Voice, SMS and phone-number features

If a live plan includes voice, SMS, Wi-Fi calling, voicemail, local-number support or number portability, those features will be listed. If a plan is data-only, it will be clearly labelled data-only rather than implying conventional phone service.

Roaming and international use

Streetwise will identify whether domestic roaming or international roaming is supported, which destinations or zones are included where applicable, the relevant data, voice or SMS allowances, and any separate rates or restrictions.

Device and eSIM requirements

Streetwise will state the key compatibility requirements for supported devices and eSIM activation. Compatibility tools and provider checks can reduce errors, but a compatibility result is not a guarantee of identical performance in every location.

Changes to a plan

If a material plan term changes after launch, Streetwise intends to communicate the change clearly and update the public plan description so customers are not relying on obsolete marketing language.

Current development status

Streetwise is currently in launch-waitlist mode. Prices, data allowances and hotspot targets shown on the main website are planning and negotiation targets, not active cellular offers. Final live terms depend on the selected wholesale provider, written commercial approval, regulatory and tax requirements, completed provisioning tests and verified economics.

What “Clear Terms” does not mean

It does not mean every plan will have no limits, no network management or no restrictions. It means the limits that materially affect the service will be explained plainly enough for a reasonable customer to understand before purchasing.
