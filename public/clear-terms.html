Streetwise commitment

Clear Terms

Customers should know what they are buying before they pay for it.

What it means

Before Streetwise opens cellular sales, each live plan will state its actual monthly price, included high-speed data, hotspot allowance, post-threshold speed, network-management rules, roaming limits, supported services and any material fees or restrictions.

How we will describe unlimited service

If a plan uses the word “unlimited”, Streetwise will also disclose any high-speed threshold, reduced-speed policy, hotspot limit or other material network restriction that applies. The headline will not replace the important terms.

Current status

Streetwise is currently in launch-waitlist mode. The prices and allowances shown on the main site are negotiation and product targets, not active cellular offers. Final terms depend on the selected wholesale provider, written commercial approval and completed testing.

What this does not promise

Clear terms do not mean every plan will have no limits. It means material limits will be disclosed plainly rather than hidden behind vague marketing.
