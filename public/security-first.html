Streetwise commitment

Security First

Security controls should be part of the service design, not an afterthought added after launch.

What it means

Streetwise is being designed around protected account access, careful handling of credentials and customer data, controlled provider integrations, secure payment workflows and practical protections against unauthorised SIM or account changes.

SIM-swap and port-out protection

Streetwise plans to use the strongest account-recovery, SIM-change and number-port protections that the selected wholesale provider and implemented system support. Exact controls will be published only after they are contractually available and tested.

Provider and payment security

Provider API keys, payment secrets, database passwords and other sensitive credentials are not intended for public code or customer-facing pages. Production integrations remain gated until the appropriate security and commercial requirements are met.

What this does not promise

No mobile service can honestly claim that fraud, account takeover or network attacks are impossible. “Security First” means Streetwise will design for prevention, controlled access, monitoring and recovery while being clear about the limits of the available protections.
